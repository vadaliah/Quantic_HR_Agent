# POL-016 Workplace Health and Safety

This policy applies to every Acme Corp office, and to remote work settings where
the company has provided equipment. It sets out reporting duties and the
company's obligations.

## 1. Employer Duties

Acme Corp must provide a workplace that is safe and without risk to health so
far as is reasonably practicable. This includes maintaining equipment in safe
condition, providing information and training on risks, and maintaining a
written record of assessments for high-risk activities.

Managers must complete a workplace risk assessment for their area each year, and
repeat it after any significant change to the space, equipment, or work
process.

## 2. Employee Duties

Employees must take reasonable care of their own safety and of others affected
by their actions, cooperate with safety measures, and report hazards they cannot
safely resolve themselves.

Employees must not interfere with safety equipment, including fire doors,
alarms, and first-aid stations.

## 3. Incident Reporting

Every injury, near miss, and unsafe condition must be reported within 24 hours
in the safety tool. Where a person requires medical treatment, the report must be
made the same day and the local emergency number called first where there is any
question of severity.

Reports are reviewed by the safety lead within five working days. A report that
discloses a serious risk must be escalated to the People Operations director
immediately, without waiting for the five-day review.

## 4. Remote Work Settings

Where the company has provided a desk, chair, or monitor under POL-005, the
employee is responsible for setting it up as instructed. The company is not
responsible for hazards in the employee's home that it did not create and cannot
control, but will respond to a report of a defect in equipment it supplied.

An employee who is injured while working remotely must report the injury under
section 3 in the same way as an office injury.

## 5. First Aid and Emergency

Each office must have at least one trained first aider for every 50 people on
site, and a stocked first-aid kit that is checked monthly. Evacuation routes
must be displayed on each floor.

Personal emergency evacuation plans are available to any employee who needs one.
Requests are made to People Operations and are treated as confidential.

## 6. Working Hours and Fatigue

Employees must not drive or operate machinery when unfit through fatigue,
illness, or medication. Where a role involves safety-critical work, the manager
must manage rosters so that no employee works more than 12 hours in a shift
without a rest break, and must record any exception.
