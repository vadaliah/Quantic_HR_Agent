# Corpus Sources and Provenance

All documents in this corpus were **written from scratch for this project**.
They describe a fictional company, "Acme Corp", and are entirely synthetic.

## Original text

No third-party handbook text is reproduced. Public-domain and openly licensed
sources were consulted for structure and for the shape of common HR provisions,
but the wording, numbering, thresholds, and figures are original.

| Source consulted | Licence | How it informed this corpus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US Office of Personnel Management, *Guide to Telework and Remote Work in the Federal Government* (2025) | Public domain (US Government work) | Structure of POL-003 and POL-004: eligibility, approval paths, duration thresholds |
| US General Services Administration, ADM 6040.1A *Telework Policy* | Public domain (US Government work) | Restricted-location handling in POL-004 |
| US Department of Labor FMLA guidance | Public domain (US Government work) | Eligibility framing and 12-week entitlement in POL-008 |
| Clef employee handbook | CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) | Topic checklist only; no text retained |
| GitLab Handbook | CC BY-SA 4.0 | Not used. Consulted for topic coverage; no text retained |

## Notes

- No document in this corpus is copied, adapted, or derived from a copyrighted
  employee handbook.
- No real employee data appears anywhere in this repository. All names, IDs,
  balances, and records are synthetic.
- Licence statements for the two CC0 sources were checked at the time of
  writing. CC0 places the work in the public domain, so no attribution
  obligation attaches, but the sources are credited here for transparency.
