# POL-003 Remote and Hybrid Work

**Document ID:** POL-003
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corp supports flexible work where the work itself, the team's needs, and
applicable law allow it. This policy explains who may work remotely, how to
request it, and what is expected of remote employees.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Hybrid

An employee who works from an assigned Acme Corp office at least two days per
week and from an approved home location for the remainder.

### 2.2 Fully remote

An employee whose home location is their primary work location and who is not
assigned to an office. Fully remote agreements are established at hire or by
written exception under section 4.

### 2.3 Work from elsewhere

Temporary work from a location other than the employee's home location or
assigned office, for example a family member's home in another state.

## 3. Eligibility

- **Full-time employees:** eligible for a hybrid schedule from the first day of
  employment, subject to role requirements.
- **Part-time employees:** eligible for hybrid work where the role permits.
- **Contractors and agency staff:** eligibility is set in the engagement
  agreement; this policy does not extend to them.
- **Roles with on-site requirements:** lab, facilities, and hardware roles are
  designated on-site and are not eligible for hybrid work.

## 4. Approval Paths

### 4.1 Hybrid and local work from elsewhere

Approved by the employee's manager, recorded in the People Operations portal.
No end date is required for a hybrid schedule.

### 4.2 Work from another US state or territory

Up to 30 consecutive days requires manager approval only. Beyond 30 days
requires review by People Operations, because payroll registration and state tax
withholding may change.

### 4.3 International work

International work is governed by POL-004. Requests to work outside the United
States for more than 30 consecutive days require both People Operations and Legal
approval before travel is booked. Requests for more than 90 consecutive days are
normally declined, and some countries are not approved for any duration.

## 5. Expectations for Remote Employees

1. Be reachable during the team's core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 in the team's local
   time zone.
2. Attend scheduled meetings on camera where the meeting owner asks for it.
3. Maintain a workspace that meets the security requirements in POL-006.
4. Report a change of home location within five business days, as it may affect
   payroll, tax, and equipment shipping.
5. Meet the same performance, availability, and record-keeping standards as
   on-site colleagues.

## 6. Equipment and Expenses

Equipment for remote work is provided under POL-010. Home office and connectivity
costs are covered under POL-005.

## 7. Review and Change

Managers may modify or end a hybrid arrangement with 30 days' notice where
business needs change, except where an accommodation applies. Accommodation
requests are handled privately by People Operations and are not governed by this
policy alone.
