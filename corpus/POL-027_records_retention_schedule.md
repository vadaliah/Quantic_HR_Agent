# POL-027 Records Retention Schedule

**Document ID:** POL-027
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp records, all formats

This schedule sets the minimum retention period for each record type and names
the owner. Retention of employee personal data is governed by POL-017; this
document supplies the periods that policy refers to.

## 1. How to Read This Schedule

A record must be kept for at least the stated period, counted from the date in
the "counted from" column. A record subject to a legal hold must not be deleted
even where the period has expired. Where two periods apply, the longer one
governs.

Deletion happens on a schedule, not on request, so that retention is consistent
across the company.

## 6. Records With No Fixed Period

Some records are kept until a specific event rather than for a period:

1. A legal hold: kept until Legal releases it.
2. A live investigation: kept until the investigation closes, then the relevant
   row above applies.
3. A device under repair: held until returned or written off.

## 7. Destruction

Deletion is performed by the record owner, recorded in the retention log, and
confirmed in writing. A record must not be deleted by an employee acting alone,
even where the period has expired.

Where a record cannot be deleted because it is held in a backup, the backup is
not restored for the purpose of deleting the record; the deletion applies when
the backup is next refreshed.
