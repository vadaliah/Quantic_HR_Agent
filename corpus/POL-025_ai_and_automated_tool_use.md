# POL-025 AI and Automated Tool Use

**Document ID:** POL-025
**Owner:** Security
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors and agency staff

This policy covers the use of AI assistants, automated agents, and any tool that
sends company data to a service outside the company. Technical controls are in
POL-006; this policy covers what may be used and what may be sent.

## 1. Approved Tools

Only tools on the published tool register may be used for company work. The
register states, for each tool, the highest data classification it may receive.
Using a tool that is not on the register is a policy breach, even where the tool
is free or widely used.

No tool may be given Confidential or Restricted data unless the register says it
may handle that level.

## 2. What Must Not Be Entered Into Any External Tool

1. Employee records, including names with salaries, addresses, or identifiers.
2. Payroll bank details, national identifiers, or credentials of any kind.
3. Customer data, unless the tool is on the register for that purpose.
4. Contract terms, unpublished financial results, or legal advice.

Where a task needs one of these, the work is done with an approved internal tool,
or with the data removed so that no individual can be identified.

## 3. Automated Agents

An automated agent or script that acts on company systems must:

1. Run under its own identity, not a person's credentials.
2. Be registered with Security before it is enabled.
3. Be limited to the access it needs, and reviewed quarterly.
4. Stop rather than guess where it is uncertain, and log the reason.

An agent must not take an irreversible action without a human confirmation step.

## 4. Generated Content

Output from an AI tool must be reviewed before it is relied on or published. The
employee who uses the output is responsible for it, in the same way as work they
produced themselves.

Generated content must not be presented as a person's own professional judgement
where it concerns a legal, medical or financial decision.

## 5. Attribution and Disclosure

Where generated content is published outside the company, the use of an AI tool
must be disclosed in a way that is accurate and not misleading. Internal use does
not need disclosure, but the source must be recorded where the output informs a
decision that is later reviewed.

## 6. Reporting a Suspected Breach

Paste of Restricted data into an unapproved tool must be reported within
**24 hours** to Security, under POL-006. Report it even where the tool provider
states that data is not retained. Do not attempt to delete the conversation
first; the report is what matters.
