# POL-001 Annex A: Worked Accrual and Balance Examples

**Document ID:** POL-001-A
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees
**Parent policy:** POL-001 Paid Time Off and Sick Leave

This annex works through the arithmetic behind PTO balances so employees and
managers can check a balance without asking People Operations. It restates the
rules in POL-001 and does not change them.

## A.1 How a Balance Is Calculated

A balance on any given date is:

```
balance = carryover + (accrual rate x months accrued this year)
          - days taken this year - days pending approval
```

- **Carryover** is capped at 5 days (POL-001 4.1).
- **Accrual rate** is 1.25 days per month for full-time employees (POL-001 2.1).
- **Months accrued this year** runs from 1 January, or from the hire date if the
  employee started during the year.
- **Days pending** are approved requests that have not yet been taken. They
  reduce the balance immediately so the same day cannot be booked twice.

## A.2 Full-Time Employee, No Carryover

An employee hired in a prior year, with no carryover and 4 days already taken.

| Month | Months accrued | Accrued | Taken | Balance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 31 January | 1.0 | 1.25 | 0 | 1.25 |
| 28 February | 2.0 | 2.50 | 0 | 2.50 |
| 31 March | 3.0 | 3.75 | 1 | 2.75 |
| 30 April | 4.0 | 5.00 | 1 | 4.00 |
| 31 May | 5.0 | 6.25 | 2 | 4.25 |
| 30 June | 6.0 | 7.50 | 4 | 3.50 |
| 31 July | 7.0 | 8.75 | 4 | 4.75 |
| 31 August | 8.0 | 10.00 | 4 | 6.00 |
| 30 September | 9.0 | 11.25 | 4 | 7.25 |
| 31 October | 10.0 | 12.50 | 4 | 8.50 |
| 30 November | 11.0 | 13.75 | 4 | 9.75 |
| 31 December | 12.0 | 15.00 | 4 | 11.00 |

At year end the employee has 11 days. Carryover is capped at 5, so 6 days are
forfeited on 31 December and 5 days roll forward.

## A.3 Employee Hired During the Year

An employee hired on 15 September, full time, no carryover, no leave taken.

| Month | Months accrued | Accrued | Balance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 30 September | 0.5 | 0.63 | 0.63 |
| 31 October | 1.5 | 1.88 | 1.88 |
| 30 November | 2.5 | 3.13 | 3.13 |
| 31 December | 3.5 | 4.38 | 4.38 |

Accrual starts on the first day of employment (POL-001 2.1), so a mid-month
start produces a part-month figure.

## A.4 Part-Time Employee

A part-time employee scheduled for 24 hours a week accrues in proportion to a
40-hour week (POL-001 2.2):

```
24 / 40 = 0.6
1.25 x 0.6 = 0.75 days per month
```

Over a full year that is 9 days, against 15 for a full-time colleague. The
15-day entitlement is not reduced; the accrual rate is.

## A.5 Effect of a Pending Request

An employee with a 10.00-day balance requests 3 days, approved but not yet taken.

| State | Balance | Can request 3 more? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before the request | 10.00 | Yes |
| Request pending | 7.00 | Yes |
| Request taken | 7.00 | Yes |

If the same employee had a 2.00-day balance, the pending request would be
rejected at submission rather than leaving a negative balance.

## A.6 Negative and Zero Balances

A balance should never go negative. Where a record shows a negative figure, it
means days were taken before they accrued, which is not permitted. People
Operations corrects the record and the manager reviews how the absence was
approved.
