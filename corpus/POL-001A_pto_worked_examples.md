# POL-001 Annex A: Worked Accrual and Balance Examples

**Document ID:** POL-001-A
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees
**Parent policy:** POL-001 Paid Time Off and Sick Leave

This annex works through the arithmetic behind PTO balances so employees and
managers can check a balance without asking People Operations. It restates the
rules in POL-001 and does not change them. Where this annex and POL-001 differ,
POL-001 governs.

## A.1 How a Balance Is Calculated

A balance on any given date is:

    accrued to date + carryover - taken this year - pending = available

Full-time employees accrue **15 days** a year, which is **1.25 days per completed
month of service** (POL-001 2.1). Accrual starts on the first day of employment.
A maximum of **5 days** of unused vacation carries into the following year;
anything above that is forfeited on 31 December (POL-001 4.1).

Part-time employees scheduled for fewer than **30 hours** a week accrue pro rata
(POL-001 2.2).

## A.2 Worked Example: An Eight-Month Balance

An employee hired on 1 March has completed eight months by 31 October.

| Step | Figure | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Months completed | 8 | — |
| Accrued (8 x 1.25) | 10 days | POL-001 2.1 |
| Carryover from last year | 4 days | POL-001 4.1 |
| Taken this year | 6 days | — |
| Pending requests | 2 days | — |
| **Available** | **6 days** | 10 + 4 - 6 - 2 |

The employee can take **6 days**. A request for 3 days or more needs manager
approval before the time is taken (POL-001 3.1); a request for fewer than 3 days
is self-service provided the balance covers it and no blackout date applies
(POL-002 5).

## A.3 Worked Example: Carryover at Year End

An employee ends the year with 9 days unused.

| Step | Figure | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unused at 31 December | 9 days | — |
| Maximum carryover | 5 days | POL-001 4.1 |
| Forfeited | 4 days | 9 - 5 |
| Balance on 1 January | 5 days | — |

The 4 days above the cap are forfeited and cannot be paid out (POL-001 4.1).
Nothing in this annex allows an exception to the cap; where local law requires
carryover beyond it, People Operations applies the local rule instead.

## A.4 Worked Example: A Request Above the Balance

An employee with 6 available days requests 8 days.

| Step | Figure | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requested | 8 days | — |
| Available | 6 days | A.1 |
| Shortfall | 2 days | 8 - 6 |
| Approved? | No, as submitted | — |

The request is not approved as submitted. The employee may split it - 6 days of
PTO plus 2 days of unpaid leave with manager approval - or reduce the request to
6 days. Unpaid leave for a short period does not require a formal leave of
absence under POL-008.

## A.5 Accrual During Leave

Accrual pauses during unpaid leave and continues only where required by law
(POL-001 2.3). An employee returning from 4 months of unpaid leave does not
accrue the 5 days that would otherwise have accrued in that period. Accrual
resumes on the first day back.

## A.6 Sick Leave Is Separate

Sick leave accrues at **1 hour per 30 hours worked**, up to a maximum balance of
**5 days** (POL-001 5.1). It is not drawn from the vacation balance in A.1, it
does not carry over between years, and it is not paid out at separation. A
question about a vacation balance should not be answered from a sick-leave
figure.
