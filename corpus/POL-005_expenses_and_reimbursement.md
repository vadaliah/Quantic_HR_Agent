# POL-005 Expenses and Reimbursement

**Document ID:** POL-005
**Owner:** Finance
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees

## 1. General Principles

Acme Corp reimburses reasonable, necessary, and documented business expenses.
Employees should spend company money as they would their own, and should obtain
manager approval in advance for any single expense above **$1000**.

## 2. Home Office and Remote Work Expenses

### 2.1 One-time home office stipend

New employees who are approved for fully remote or primary-hybrid work receive a
one-time stipend of **$500** toward a desk, chair, monitor, or lighting. The
stipend is claimed within the first 90 days of employment and is
reported once, not renewable annually.

### 2.2 Monthly connectivity allowance

Fully remote employees may claim up to **$50 per month** toward home internet,
submitted quarterly with a receipt.

### 2.3 What is not reimbursed

Furniture beyond the stipend, rent or mortgage costs, and general household
utilities such as electricity and water are not reimbursed.

## 3. Equipment

Computers are issued by IT under POL-010, not purchased and expensed. Peripheral
equipment under $150 may be expensed with manager approval; anything above that
is ordered through IT.

## 4. Travel

### 4.1 Booking

Travel is booked through the Acme Corp travel tool at least 14 days in advance
where practicable. Economy class is standard for flights under six hours;
premium economy may be approved for flights over six hours with director
approval.

### 4.2 Lodging and meals

Lodging is reimbursed up to **$250 per night** in the United States and
**$350 per night** internationally. Meals are reimbursed up to **$75 per day**
domestically and **$110 per day** internationally, including tip.

### 4.3 Ground transport

Rideshare, taxi, and public transport are reimbursable for business travel.
Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate.
Parking and tolls are reimbursable with a receipt.

### 4.4 Not reimbursable

Personal travel extensions, seat upgrades outside section 4.1, minibar and
in-room entertainment, traffic fines, and travel insurance purchased for personal
reasons.

## 5. Submitting a Claim

1. Submit within **30 days** of the expense date.
2. Attach an itemized receipt for any expense of **$25** or more.
3. Allocate the expense to a cost centre or project code.
4. Manager approval is required for every claim.

Claims submitted later than 30 days may be declined. Reimbursement
is issued with the next payroll cycle after approval.

## 6. Corporate Card

Employees issued a corporate card must reconcile charges within 14 days of the
statement date. Personal charges on a corporate card must be repaid within 10
days.

## 7. Non-Compliance

Submitting a false or inflated claim is a serious matter and may lead to
corrective action including termination and recovery of funds.
