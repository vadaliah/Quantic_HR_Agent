# POL-023 Learning and Professional Development

**Document ID:** POL-023
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees

This policy covers study support, certification, and how development spending is
approved. Where a course is bought as an expense, POL-005 applies; this policy
covers what the company will fund and on what conditions.

## 1. Study Support

Employees may claim up to **$1,500 per year** toward courses, certifications and
conferences relevant to their current role or to a role the company expects them
to move into. Costs above $1,500 may be approved case by case by the department
director.

The annual amount does not accumulate. Unused study support is not carried into
the following year and is not paid out at separation.

## 2. Approval

Requests are made to the manager before booking, with the cost, the dates, and
how the learning applies to the role. Approval must be recorded in the learning
tool; a verbal agreement is not sufficient for a claim.

A request that takes the employee away from work for more than **5 working days**
in a year requires director approval, and the team's coverage must be agreed in
advance.

## 3. Time Off for Study

Study time during working hours is agreed with the manager and recorded like any
other absence. Time spent studying outside working hours is not working time and
does not attract overtime under POL-018.

Where a course requires an exam during working hours, the employee is given the
time off without using PTO, provided the request was approved under section 2.

## 4. Certification

Where a certification is required for the role, the company pays for the course,
the exam, and one resit. The employee must take the exam within **90 days** of
finishing the course.

Two failures of the same required certification are reviewed with the manager and
People Operations before a third attempt is funded.

## 5. Repayment

Where the company funds learning costing more than **$1,000**, and the employee
leaves within **12 months** of completing it, the company may seek repayment on a
pro-rata basis, where local law permits and where the employee agreed in writing
before the course began.

Repayment must not be sought where the employee leaves because of redundancy, or
where the role was made redundant, or where the employee is unable to complete
the course for a reason outside their control.

## 6. Sharing the Learning

An employee who attends a conference or completes a certification is expected to
share the main points with their team, in a short session or a written summary,
within 30 days.

This is an expectation, not a condition of the funding, and it is not a reason to
refuse a claim.
