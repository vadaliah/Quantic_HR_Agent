# POL-013 Recruitment and Hiring

This policy sets out how Acme Corp hires, from requisition to offer. It applies to
all roles and all locations unless a country addendum says otherwise.

## 1. Requisition and Approval

Hiring managers must raise a requisition in the recruitment tool before any
candidate contact. A requisition requires approval from the hiring manager's
director and from Finance. Requisitions for headcount that is not in the
approved annual plan require additional approval from the People Operations
director.

Requisitions remain open for 90 days. A requisition that has not been filled
within 90 days must be re-approved before further interviews are scheduled.

## 2. Interview Process

Every hiring process must include at least two interviewers who have completed
interview training within the last 12 months. At least one interviewer must be
from outside the hiring team, so that no hiring decision rests on a single
manager's judgement.

The standard process is a screening call, a skills interview, and a values
interview. Roles at manager level and above add a panel interview and a
reference check. Panel interviews must include a member of People Operations.

Candidates must not be asked about age, marital status, religion, disability,
national origin, or salary history. Where a candidate volunteers information
that a protected characteristic prevents them from completing a standard
exercise, the exercise must be adjusted.

## 3. Assessment and Notes

Interviewers record structured notes against the published competencies within
24 hours of the interview. Free-text impressions that are not tied to a
competency are not admissible in the hiring decision.

Scorecards use a four-point scale. A candidate must not advance with a score
below 3 in any required competency. Decisions to advance are made by the hiring
manager and recorded in the recruitment tool.

## 4. Offers

Offers are issued in writing by People Operations only. A verbal offer has no
effect. Offers are conditional on a background check where the role involves
access to financial systems, personal data, or customer funds.

Offers to candidates who will work outside the country of the hiring entity
require review by People Operations and Legal before issue, because of
employment and tax registration requirements.

## 5. Referrals

Employees may refer candidates for open requisitions. A successful referral is
paid $2,000 after the referred employee has completed 90 days of service. A
referral is not eligible for payment where the referring employee is on the
hiring panel; see POL-007 for the benefits treatment of referral payments.

## 6. Internal Moves

Employees may apply for internal roles after 12 months in their current role,
unless their manager waives the requirement in writing. A manager may not block
an internal application, but may require up to 30 days' notice before the move
takes effect.

## 7. Records and Retention

Interview notes and scorecards are retained for 24 months after the
requisition closes, then deleted. Candidates may request deletion of their data
under POL-006.
