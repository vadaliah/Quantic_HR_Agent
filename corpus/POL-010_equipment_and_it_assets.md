# POL-010 Equipment and IT Assets

**Document ID:** POL-010
**Owner:** IT
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees and contractors

## 1. Purpose

This policy covers the issue, use, care, and return of Acme Corp equipment, and
the support available to employees working remotely under POL-003.

## 2. Standard Issue

### 2.1 Full-time employees

| Item | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Laptop | Company-issued, replaced every 3 years |
| Monitor | One, on request |
| Headset | One, replaced every 2 years |
| Peripherals | Keyboard and mouse on request |

### 2.2 Contractors

Contractors normally use their own equipment and receive access to company
systems through the contractor tenant. Any company-issued item is recorded in the
asset register and returned at the end of the engagement.

## 3. Refresh Cycle

Laptops are refreshed on a **3-year cycle**. An earlier replacement
may be approved where a repair is not economical, where the role's compute needs
have materially changed, or where the device no longer meets security
requirements.

## 4. Care and Use

1. Equipment is for company work, with incidental personal use permitted.
2. Devices must run the company management agent and full-disk encryption.
3. Screen lock must engage after five minutes of inactivity.
4. Do not install software that is not on the approved list; use the self-service
   catalogue in the IT portal.
5. Do not attempt repairs yourself. Contact the service desk.

## 5. Remote Work Support

Employees working from home may contact the service desk during business hours.
IT will ship replacement equipment to an approved home address. Employees working
from another country under POL-004 should allow additional time and should not
take company equipment to a restricted location.

## 6. Loss, Theft, and Damage

Report lost, stolen, or damaged equipment to IT and Security within **24 hours**.
A police report is required for theft. Accidental damage is covered once per year
without charge; repeated damage may be recovered from the employee where permitted
by law.

## 7. Return of Assets

All equipment must be returned within **10 business days** of the last working
day, or of the start of an unpaid leave of absence exceeding 90 days. Employees
who do not return equipment may have the value recovered where permitted by law.
