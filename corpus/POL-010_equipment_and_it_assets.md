# POL-010 Equipment and IT Assets

**Document ID:** POL-010
**Owner:** IT
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees and contractors

## 1. Purpose

This policy covers the issue, use, care, and return of Acme Corp equipment, and
the support available to employees working remotely under POL-003.

## 2. Standard Issue

### 2.1 Full-time employees

| Item | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Laptop | Company-issued, replaced every 3 years |
| Monitor | One, on request |
| Headset | One, replaced every 2 years |
| Peripherals | Keyboard and mouse on request |

### 2.2 Contractors

Contractors normally use their own equipment and receive access to company
systems through the contractor tenant. Any company-issued item is recorded in the
asset register and returned at the end of the engagement.

## 3. Refresh Cycle

Laptops are refreshed on a **3-year cycle**. An earlier replacement
may be approved where a repair is not economical, where the role's compute needs
have materially changed, or where the device no longer meets security
requirements.

## 4. Care and Use

1. Equipment is for company work, with incidental personal use permitted.
2. Devices must run the company management agent and full-disk encryption.
3. Screen lock must engage after five minutes of inactivity.
4. Do not install software that is not on the approved list; use the self-service
   catalogue in the IT portal.
5. Do not attempt repairs yourself. Contact the service desk.

## 5. Remote Work Support

Employees working from home may contact the service desk during business hours.
IT will ship replacement equipment to an approved home address. Employees working
from another country under POL-004 should allow additional time and should not
take company equipment to a restricted location.

## 6. Loss, Theft, and Damage

Replacement of a personal device is not reimbursed. Report lost, stolen, or damaged equipment to IT and Security within **24 hours**.
A police report is required for theft. Accidental damage is covered once per year
without charge; repeated damage may be recovered from the employee where permitted
by law.

## 7. Return of Assets

All equipment must be returned within **10 business days** of the last working
day, or of the start of an unpaid leave of absence exceeding 90 days. Employees
who do not return equipment may have the value recovered where permitted by law.

## 8. Procurement and Purchasing

Standard items in section 2 are ordered through the self-service catalogue and
need no separate approval. Anything outside the catalogue requires IT approval
and follows the expense rules in POL-005:

- Items under **$150** may be expensed by the employee and claimed under POL-005.
- Items above **$150** are purchased by IT on a purchase order and are not
  reimbursed to the employee. This replaces the reimbursement route in POL-005
  for equipment, so that assets are recorded in the asset register.
- Items above **$1,000** require IT and Finance approval before purchase.

Employees must not purchase equipment and claim reimbursement where this section
routes the purchase through IT. A purchase made through IT appears on a purchase
order and produces no receipt for the employee to claim.

## 9. Personal Devices

Company systems may be reached from a personal device only through the managed
access client, and only where the device meets the same requirements as section 4
for encryption and screen lock. The company does not reimburse personal devices
and does not repair them.

A personal device used for company work must be enrolled in the management agent
and may be subject to a remote wipe **of company data only** on loss, theft, or
the employee's last working day. The employee must be told this before enrolling,
and consent is recorded.

Section 4 (screen lock after five minutes, full-disk encryption) applies to a
personal device the same way it applies to a company device. Support for a
personal device is limited to the managed access client; the company does not
diagnose or repair the device itself.

## 10. Software and Licences

Only software on the approved list may be installed on a company device.
Licences are held by the company; an employee must not buy a licence personally
and claim it as an expense where it is already on the approved list.

Where a licence is required for a role, the manager requests it through IT and
the cost is charged to the department cost centre, not to the employee.

## 11. Offboarding and Asset Return

Access is removed on the last working day. The asset register is reconciled
within **5 business days** of the last working day, and the employee receives a
written confirmation that their assets are cleared.

Where equipment is not returned within the period in section 7, the value may be
recovered under POL-018 and the employee is notified before any deduction.
