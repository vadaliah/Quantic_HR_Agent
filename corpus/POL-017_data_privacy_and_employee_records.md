# POL-017 Data Privacy and Employee Records

This policy covers personal data about employees, candidates, and former
employees. Technical security controls are in POL-006; this policy covers
privacy, retention, and individual rights.

## 1. Lawful Basis and Notice

Acme Corp processes employee personal data for the performance of the
employment relationship, to meet legal obligations, and for legitimate business
purposes that do not override the employee's interests. Employees are given a
privacy notice on hire, and again whenever the purposes of processing change.

Data must not be collected for one purpose and then used for an unrelated one
without notice to the employee.

## 2. Access and Confidentiality

Access to employee records is limited to those who need it for their role.
Managers may see their own team's job, location, and contact data; they do not
have access to medical information, benefits elections, or salary history.

Ask People Operations for any record outside your role's need. Do not ask
colleagues with system access to look up a record informally; a request must be
logged with a business reason.

## 3. Retention

Employee records are retained for the employment period plus seven years, unless
a shorter period is required or a legal hold applies. Recruitment records are
retained for 24 months under POL-013. Records subject to a legal hold must not
be deleted even where the standard period has expired.

Deletion is performed on a schedule, not on request, so that retention is
consistent. A deletion request is forwarded to the privacy lead.

## 4. Employee Rights

Employees may request a copy of their personal data, ask for corrections, and
object to a processing activity. Requests are answered within 30 days of receipt
where the request is clear; the privacy lead may extend this once where the
request is complex, and must tell the employee if so.

Corrections to factual errors are applied by People Operations. A correction
does not remove a historical record where the record itself is accurate.

## 5. Sharing and Transfers

Personal data may be shared with a payroll provider, a benefits administrator, or
a background-check provider under a written agreement. Data must not be shared
with a third party without an agreement that limits the use to the stated
purpose.

Cross-border transfers require a lawful transfer mechanism and review by Legal.
Transfers to a country without adequate protection must be documented before the
transfer begins.

## 6. Breach

A suspected personal-data breach must be reported to Security and the privacy
lead within 24 hours of discovery, in line with POL-006. The privacy lead
assesses whether the affected individuals and any regulator must be told. Do not
contact affected individuals, or make any public statement, before that
assessment is complete.
