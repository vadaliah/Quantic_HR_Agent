# POL-009A Onboarding Checklist Annex

This annex provides the operational checklist referenced by POL-009. It is
written for the hiring manager and the buddy, and is not a substitute for the
policy.

## A.1 Before the Start Date

| Item | Owner | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signed offer and contract returned | People Operations | 10 days before start |
| Background check completed where required | People Operations | Before start |
| Equipment ordered | IT | 10 days before start |
| Payroll record created | Payroll | 5 days before start |
| Buddy assigned | Hiring manager | 5 days before start |
| Calendar and system access requested | IT | 3 days before start |

## A.2 Day One

- Welcome from the manager, covering the role, the team, and the first 30 days.
- Issue of equipment and confirmation that the employee can log in.
- Completion of the identity and right-to-work check, where not already done.
- Introduction to the buddy and to the immediate team.
- Reading of the code of conduct acknowledgement in POL-011.

## A.3 First Week

- Policy acknowledgements completed: POL-006, POL-011, POL-017.
- Security training booked; it must be completed within 30 days of the start
  date under POL-006.
- Role goals drafted with the manager; finalised within the first 30 days.
- Benefits enrolment opened; enrolment must be completed within 30 days of the
  start date under POL-007.

## A.4 First 30 Days

- Manager and employee meet weekly. The manager records a short note after each
  meeting.
- The employee confirms they have the access needed for the role; missing access
  is raised with IT rather than worked around.
- Any equipment defect reported under POL-010.

## A.5 Days 31 to 90

- Probation review at day 60, covering progress against the role goals.
- Final probation decision no later than day 90, under POL-009. An extension,
  where granted, must be recorded in writing before day 90.
- Home office stipend claim, where the employee is eligible, must be submitted
  within 90 days of the start date under POL-005.

## A.6 Completion

The checklist is complete when every row above has an owner and a date
recorded. The hiring manager is responsible for recording completion in the
onboarding tool.
