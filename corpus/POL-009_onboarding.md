# POL-009 Onboarding

**Document ID:** POL-009
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All new Acme Corp employees

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the onboarding steps for a new employee, the responsibilities
of the manager and People Operations, and the milestones in the first
90 days.

## 2. Before the Start Date

### 2.1 People Operations

1. Issue the offer letter and collect the signed acknowledgement.
2. Initiate the background check where the role requires one.
3. Create the employee record and confirm the reporting line.
4. Send the pre-start pack: equipment, first-day logistics, and system access
   requests.

### 2.2 Manager

1. Confirm the team's first-week plan and assign an onboarding buddy.
2. Request system access at least three business days before the start date.
3. Prepare the first project brief.

## 3. Day One

- Collect the laptop and complete device setup with IT under POL-010.
- Complete security training and enable multi-factor authentication under POL-006.
- Review this policy and the Code of Conduct, POL-011.
- Meet the manager and the onboarding buddy.

## 4. First 90 Days

| Milestone | By day |
| --- | --- |
| Security training complete | 5 |
| Benefits enrolment completed | 30 |
| Role expectations and 30/60/90 plan agreed | 30 |
| First performance check-in | 90 |

Probation is 90 days by default and may be extended once by written
notice. Benefits eligibility is not affected by probation; see POL-007.

## 5. System Access and Accounts

Access is least-privilege and role-based, following POL-006. Managers must
request additions rather than sharing credentials. Accounts are disabled on the
employee's last working day.

## 6. Buddy Programme

Every new employee is assigned a buddy for the first 60 days. The buddy is a peer
who is not the manager, and answers day-to-day questions about tools, process,
and culture.

## 7. Feedback

New employees complete a short onboarding survey at day 30 and day 90. Feedback is
reviewed by People Operations each quarter and used to revise this policy.
