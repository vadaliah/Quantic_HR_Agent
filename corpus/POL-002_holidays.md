# POL-002 Company Holidays

**Document ID:** POL-002
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees

## 1. Observed Holidays

Acme Corp observes the following holidays each year. Offices are closed and
employees receive holiday pay.

| Holiday | Date |
| --- | --- |
| New Year's Day | 1 January |
| Martin Luther King Jr. Day | Third Monday in January |
| Presidents' Day | Third Monday in February |
| Memorial Day | Last Monday in May |
| Juneteenth | 19 June |
| Independence Day | 4 July |
| Labor Day | First Monday in September |
| Thanksgiving Day | Fourth Thursday in November |
| Day after Thanksgiving | Fourth Friday in November |
| Winter Holiday | 24 December through 26 December |

That is **eleven paid holidays** per year. Where a holiday falls on a Saturday it
is observed on the preceding Friday; where it falls on a Sunday it is observed on
the following Monday.

## 2. Floating Holidays

Full-time employees receive **two floating holidays** per calendar year, taken on
dates that matter to the employee and recorded in the People Operations portal.
Floating holidays do not carry over and are not paid out at separation.

## 3. Eligibility

Holiday pay is available to full-time and part-time regular employees.
Independent contractors and agency-provided staff are not eligible for holiday
pay, and should follow the schedule in their engagement agreement.

## 4. Working on a Holiday

### 4.1 Pre-approval

An employee who works on an observed holiday must have the arrangement approved
by their manager at least five business days in advance, except for on-call
rotation or an operational emergency.

### 4.2 Compensation

Approved holiday work is compensated at the employee's regular rate plus a
substitute day off, taken within 60 days. Holiday work is not paid at a premium
rate unless a site-specific agreement provides one.

## 5. Office Closure Between Holidays

When a holiday falls on a Tuesday or Thursday, the adjacent Monday or Friday is
not an automatic closure. Employees who wish to take the adjacent day should use
PTO under POL-001 or a floating holiday.
