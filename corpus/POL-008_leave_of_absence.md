# POL-008 Leave of Absence

**Document ID:** POL-008
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees

## 1. Purpose

This policy covers extended leave beyond ordinary PTO and sick leave, including
family and medical leave, parental leave, and personal leave of absence.

## 2. Parental Leave

### 2.1 Entitlement

An eligible employee who becomes a parent through birth, adoption, or placement
of a foster child receives **12 weeks of fully paid parental leave**, taken
within 12 months of the birth or placement.

### 2.2 Eligibility

Employees must have completed at least 90 days of continuous
employment. Employees in the first 90 days receive statutory leave
where required by law and unpaid leave for the remainder.

### 2.3 Intermittent use

Parental leave may be taken in up to three blocks with manager approval.

## 3. Family and Medical Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement

Employees who have worked at least 12 months and 1,250 hours in the prior year
are eligible for up to **12 weeks of job-protected leave** in a rolling 12-month
period for the employee's own serious health condition or that of a spouse,
child, or parent.

### 3.2 Compensation

This leave runs concurrently with pay available under any statutory program or
state insurance benefit. Where statutory pay is less than the employee's base
pay, Acme Corp does not top up the difference unless required by law.

### 3.3 Returning to work

Employees return to the same or an equivalent role with equivalent pay and
benefits. Employees must confirm their intended return date in writing at least
10 business days in advance.

## 4. Personal Leave of Absence

### 4.1 Unpaid personal leave

After 12 months of service, an employee may request up to **4 weeks of unpaid
personal leave** in a rolling 12-month period, subject to director approval.
Approval considers team coverage and the reason for the request.

### 4.2 Sabbatical

After **5 years of continuous service**, an employee may request a
**4-week paid sabbatical**, in addition to ordinary PTO. A sabbatical must be
requested at least 90 days in advance.

## 5. Requesting Leave

1. Notify your manager and People Operations as soon as practicable, and at least
   30 days in advance where the need is foreseeable.
2. Submit the request in the People Operations portal with dates and, where
   required, supporting documentation.
3. People Operations confirms eligibility, pay treatment, and the effect on
   benefits within five business days.
4. People Operations notifies the manager of the approved dates and coverage plan.

## 6. Benefits During Leave

Medical coverage continues during paid leave. During unpaid leave, the employee's
share of premiums remains payable; coverage may be cancelled for non-payment
after 30 days' notice.

## 7. Return and Non-Return

Employees who do not return at the end of an approved leave and do not request an
extension may be treated as a voluntary resignation. Equipment must be returned
under POL-010.
