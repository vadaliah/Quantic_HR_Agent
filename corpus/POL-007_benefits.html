POL-007 Benefits

Document ID: POL-007
Owner: People Operations
Applies to: Full-time employees

1. Eligibility

1.1 Who is eligible

Full-time regular employees scheduled for 30 or more hours per week are eligible for the benefits described in this policy.

1.2 Who is not eligible

Independent contractors, temporary workers, and staff provided by an agency are not eligible for medical, dental, vision, retirement, or wellness benefits. Part-time employees scheduled for fewer than 30 hours per week are eligible for retirement contributions only.

1.3 Waiting period

Coverage begins on the first day of the month following the employee's start date. Dependents may be enrolled at the same time.

2. Medical, Dental, and Vision

2.1 Plan tiers

Three tiers are offered: Core, Standard, and Premium. Acme Corp contributes 85% of the premium for employee-only coverage on all tiers, and 40% of the additional premium for dependent coverage.

2.2 Choosing above the baseline

Where an employee selects a tier more expensive than Core, the employee pays the difference shown in the enrolment tool.

2.3 Waiver allowance

An employee who has coverage elsewhere and waives medical coverage receives a taxable allowance of $25 per month.

3. Retirement

Acme Corp offers a 401(k) plan with a company match of 100% of the first 4% of eligible pay. Vesting in the match is immediate. Employees may change their contribution rate at any time through the plan provider.

4. Disability and Life Insurance

Short-term and long-term disability insurance is provided at no cost to eligible employees. Basic life and accidental death and dismemberment coverage is provided at one times annual earnings, with age-based reductions beginning at age 65.

5. Wellness and Learning

5.1 Wellness

Employees receive $600 per year toward gym membership, fitness classes, or a wellness app, claimed quarterly.

5.2 Continuing education

Employees may claim up to $1,500 per year for courses, conferences, and certification exams related to their role, with manager approval. Costs above $1,500 may be approved case by case.

5.3 Referral bonus

Employees receive $2,000 for a successful referral, paid 90 days after the referred person's start date. Referrals are not available for roles the employee manages.

6. Enrolment and Life Events

Enrolment is completed within 30 days of the start date or of a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth, or loss of other coverage. Outside that window, changes wait until the next annual enrolment period.
