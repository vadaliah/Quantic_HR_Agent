# POL-021 Contractor and Agency Worker Engagement

**Document ID:** POL-021
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All contractors, agency-provided staff, and their engagement owners

This policy covers how contractors and agency workers are engaged and what they
are entitled to. It exists because several other policies turn on employment
type, and a question about a contractor's entitlement is usually answered by two
documents together.

## 1. Classification

A worker is either an employee, a contractor, or agency-provided staff. The
classification is set at engagement and recorded in the HR system. It is not
changed by the length of the engagement, the number of hours worked, or by who
directs the day-to-day work.

Misclassification carries tax and legal consequences. Anyone who believes a
worker is classified wrongly must raise it with People Operations rather than
recording a different type locally.

## 2. What Contractors Are Entitled To

| Entitlement | Employee | Contractor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid vacation accrual | Yes | No |
| Paid holidays | Yes | No |
| Sick leave | Yes | No |
| Medical, dental, vision | Yes | No |
| Retirement contribution | Yes | No |
| Equipment issued by IT | Yes | Limited, on request |
| Expense reimbursement | Yes | Yes, with engagement-owner approval |
| Access to company systems | Yes | Through the contractor tenant |
| Security training | Yes | Yes, mandatory |

Contractors are paid for time worked under the terms of their engagement
agreement. Paid time off is a matter for that agreement, not for POL-001.

## 3. Agency-Provided Staff

Agency staff are engaged through the agency and are not Acme Corp workers for
the purposes of any policy in this corpus. Questions about pay, hours, or
holidays are answered by the agency.

Agency staff may be given system access for the duration of the placement, and
must complete security training under POL-006. They may not be given access to
Confidential or Restricted data without the engagement owner's written approval.

## 4. Engagement Owner Responsibilities

Every contractor and agency placement has a named engagement owner. The owner
must:

1. Confirm the classification before the start date.
2. Provide the access the role needs, and no more.
3. Review access every quarter, with Security.
4. Notify People Operations and Security before the end date, so access is
   removed on the last day.

An engagement owner who is unsure whether a worker may be given a system or a
data set should treat it as not permitted and ask Security.

## 5. Converting to Employment

A contractor may apply for an open role and is treated as any other candidate
under POL-013. Where a contractor is hired as an employee, the start date of
employment is the first day as an employee, not the start of the contract. There
is no automatic conversion and no expectation that an engagement will become
employment.

## 6. Ending an Engagement

The engagement owner records the end date in advance. Access is removed on the
last day. Equipment issued under POL-010 is returned within the period in that
policy. Final payment follows the engagement agreement, not POL-018.
