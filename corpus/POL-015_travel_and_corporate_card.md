# POL-015 Travel and Corporate Card

This policy covers travel booked for company purposes and the use of the
corporate card. Expense limits themselves are in POL-005; this policy covers
booking rules, safety, and card conduct.

## 1. Booking

Travel is booked through the Acme Corp travel tool. Bookings made outside the
tool are reimbursed only where the tool cannot serve the route or where an
emergency required an immediate change, and must be justified in the claim.

Bookings must be made at least 14 days before departure where the trip is
foreseeable. Where a trip is booked inside 14 days, the booking requires
director approval and the reason must be recorded.

Economy class is standard for flights under six hours. Premium economy is
permitted for flights over six hours. Business class requires director approval
and is limited to flights over ten hours.

## 2. Ground Transport

Public transport and ride-share are preferred where safe and practical. Rental
cars require manager approval and are permitted only where public transport is
not practical. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the published mileage rate
in POL-005.

## 3. Duty of Care

Employees travelling internationally must register the trip in the travel tool
so that People Operations can reach them in an emergency. Registration must be
completed before departure.

Employees must not travel to a location subject to a company travel
restriction. The current restricted list is maintained by Security; see POL-006
for reporting duties where a security incident occurs during travel.

Travel for more than 90 consecutive days outside the assigned location is not
travel for the purposes of this policy; it is remote work abroad and follows
POL-003 and POL-004.

## 4. Corporate Card

The corporate card is issued for company expenses only. Personal charges on the
card must be repaid within 10 days of the statement date; repeated personal use
is grounds for card withdrawal.

Cardholders must reconcile charges within 14 days of the statement date.
Receipts are stored in the expense tool. A card that is lost or stolen must be
reported to Security and to the card provider within 24 hours of discovery.

The card must not be used for cash advances, for the purchase of gift cards, or
for expenses that POL-005 states are not reimbursable.

## 5. Claims Connected to Travel

A claim for travel must include the itinerary, the purpose of the trip, and the
cost centre. Claims are submitted within 30 days of the expense date under
POL-005, and are approved by the cost centre owner.
