# POL-030 Workplace Accommodation

**Document ID:** POL-030
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees, candidates and contractors

This policy covers adjustments made so that a person can do their job, take part
in hiring, or return to work. Health and safety obligations are in POL-016;
medical information handling is in POL-017.

## 1. What an Accommodation Is

An accommodation is a change to the way work is done, the environment, or the
equipment used, so that a person with a disability or a health condition can
work on equal terms. It is not a change to the requirements of the role.

An accommodation may be temporary or permanent, and may be reviewed. A temporary
accommodation made for a short-term condition does not need to become permanent.

## 2. Examples

| Type | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Equipment | Adaptive keyboard, larger monitor, screen reader, sit-stand desk |
| Schedule | Adjusted start time, additional breaks, reduced hours temporarily |
| Location | A quieter workspace, or remote work under POL-003 |
| Process | Written rather than verbal instructions, extra time for a task |
| Environment | Noise reduction, lighting change, physical access change |

This list is not exhaustive and is not a menu: the accommodation is whatever
removes the barrier in the individual case.

## 3. Requesting an Accommodation

A request is made to the manager or directly to People Operations. The employee
does not have to disclose a diagnosis, and a request must not be refused because
medical detail was not provided.

People Operations may ask for information from a medical professional where the
limitation or the adjustment needed is not clear. Any such information is
Confidential or Restricted under POL-006 and is held separately from the
personnel file.

## 4. Handling a Request

| Stage | Owner | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acknowledged | Manager or People Operations | 2 working days |
| Discussed with the employee | Manager, with People Operations | 10 working days |
| Decision communicated | People Operations | 15 working days |
| Equipment ordered, where needed | IT | 10 working days after approval |

Where the request cannot be answered in 15 working days, the employee is told why
and given a date. Delay is not a refusal.

## 5. Cost and Undue Hardship

The company pays for the accommodation. Cost alone is not a reason to refuse
where the adjustment is required; refusal requires that the adjustment would
impose a disproportionate burden on the company, and that assessment is made by
People Operations with the department director, not by the manager alone.

Where one adjustment would impose a disproportionate burden, the company must
consider whether another adjustment removes the same barrier before refusing.

## 6. Confidentiality and Records

The fact of a request, the medical information, and the accommodation are held
separately from the personnel file and are visible only to People Operations and
those who must act on it. A manager is told what the adjustment is, not why it is
needed.

Records are retained for **7 years** from the end of the arrangement under
POL-027.

## 7. Return to Work

An employee returning from leave under POL-008 may request a phased return, a
temporary change to duties, or equipment. A phased return is agreed before the
return date where possible, and the arrangement is reviewed after 4 weeks.

An employee must not be dismissed for requesting an accommodation or for taking
leave under POL-008.
