# POL-022 Business Continuity and Emergency Response

**Document ID:** POL-022
**Owner:** Security
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees and offices

This policy covers what happens when normal operations are interrupted: by a
system outage, a natural event, a security incident, or a loss of access to a
building. Workplace safety itself is in POL-016.

## 1. Incident Levels

| Level | Meaning | Who is told | Response time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Local or single-team disruption | The team and their manager | Same working day |
| Level 2 | A system or office is unavailable | Department head and Security | Within 4 hours |
| Level 3 | Company-wide impact, or a data breach | Executive team and Security | Immediately |

A level is set by Security, not by the team that reports it. A level can be
raised at any time and is only lowered by Security.

## 2. Reporting

Any disruption is reported to Security as soon as it is noticed. Do not wait to
establish the cause, and do not attempt to fix a Level 3 incident yourself.

Where an incident involves personal data, the privacy lead must be told within 24
hours under POL-017. Where it involves a lost or compromised device, POL-006
applies.

## 3. Loss of Access to a Building

If an office cannot be occupied, the default is remote work under POL-003,
provided the team can reach the systems they need. If they cannot, work stops
rather than moving to an unapproved location.

Company equipment must not be moved to a restricted location under POL-004 to
work around a closure.

## 4. System Outage

Where a required system is unavailable, the team records the work that is
blocked and notifies the system owner. Work that does not depend on the system
continues.

Employees must not create workarounds that bypass a security control in order to
keep working. A workaround needs Security's approval before it is used, even
temporarily.

## 5. Communication

Security owns communication during an incident. Updates are issued at least
every 4 hours for a Level 2 incident and every hour for a Level 3. Individual
employees must not post about an incident on public channels, and must not
contact affected individuals, customers, or regulators directly.

## 6. Returning to Normal

An incident is closed when Security confirms that normal service has resumed and
the cause has been recorded. A review is held within 10 working days for any
Level 2 or Level 3 incident, covering what happened, what was done, and what
changes are needed.

Reviews are blameless. The purpose is to find the gap that allowed the incident,
not to assign fault.

## 7. Testing

Security runs a continuity test at least once a year, covering loss of a system
and loss of a building. Teams named in the test must take part. Results and
actions are reported to the executive team.
