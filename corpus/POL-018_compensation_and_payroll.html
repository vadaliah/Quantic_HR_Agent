POL-018 Compensation and Payroll

This policy covers pay cycles, pay changes, and deductions. Expense and travel limits are in POL-005 and POL-015. Benefits that are not pay are in POL-007.

1. Pay Cycle

Salaries are paid monthly, on the last working day of the month, by bank transfer. Where the last working day falls within a closure period, pay is released on the last working day before the closure.

Hourly and overtime-eligible employees are paid on the same monthly cycle. Overtime must be approved in advance by the manager; time worked without approval is paid where local law requires it, but is recorded as an exception.

2. Payroll Cut-off

Changes to pay, bank details, or deductions must be submitted by the 15th of the month to take effect in that month's payroll. Changes submitted after the 15th take effect in the following month. An error in an employee's favour is not recovered without the employee's agreement unless local law permits it.

3. Salary Reviews

Salary is reviewed once a year, in the April cycle. A review is not an entitlement to an increase. Reviews consider performance ratings under POL-014, the position of the role against the published salary band, and the budget available to the department.

An employee whose salary is below the minimum of their band must be brought to the band minimum at the next review, regardless of rating.

4. Job Bands

Every role maps to a band. The band defines a minimum and a maximum, and a role's band is recorded on the role profile. A salary above the band maximum is permitted only with director approval and is reviewed at each annual cycle.

5. Overtime and On-call

Overtime is paid at the published rate for roles that are overtime-eligible. On-call duty is compensated at the published on-call rate for each day of availability, whether or not the employee is called.

Managers must not ask an employee to work unpaid overtime. Where business needs require additional hours, the manager records the reason and the arrangement in the payroll tool.

6. Deductions

Deductions are made only where required by law or agreed in writing. A deduction for equipment not returned must not be made without an opportunity for the employee to return the equipment first.

Overpayments are recovered in instalments agreed with the employee. Where no agreement is reached, recovery follows local law and People Operations must be consulted before any deduction is applied.

7. Final Pay

Final pay is released on the next scheduled pay date after the last working day, and where local law sets a shorter period, that period applies. Accrued untaken vacation is paid out where required by law or where POL-001 says it is paid at separation.

Equipment must be returned within 14 days of the last working day. Access to systems is removed on the last working day; see POL-010.
