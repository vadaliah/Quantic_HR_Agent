# POL-001 Paid Time Off and Sick Leave

**Document ID:** POL-001
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-01

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets out how paid time off (PTO) and sick leave accrue, how they are
requested, and how they are approved at Acme Corp. It applies to all full-time
and part-time regular employees. Independent contractors, temporary workers, and
staff provided by an agency are not eligible for paid time off under this policy
and should consult their engagement agreement.

## 2. Vacation Accrual

### 2.1 Annual entitlement

Full-time employees accrue 15 days (1.25 days per month of completed service) of paid vacation each
calendar year. Accrual begins on the first day of employment.

### 2.2 Part-time employees

Part-time employees scheduled for fewer than 30 hours per week accrue
vacation on a prorated basis, in proportion to scheduled hours against a 40-hour
week.

### 2.3 Accrual during leave

Employees on approved leave of absence continue to accrue vacation only where
required by law. Accrual pauses during unpaid leave.

## 3. Requesting Time Off

### 3.1 Notice and approval

Employees must submit vacation requests through the People Operations portal and
notify their manager.

- Requests of **fewer than 3 days** require manager
  notification only.
- Requests of **3 days or more** require manager approval
  before any travel is booked.
- All requests should be submitted at least **5 business days** in
  advance. Requests submitted with less notice may be declined for operational
  reasons.

### 3.2 Manager decision

Managers review requests against team coverage, payroll close dates, and any
applicable blackout period. A decision is normally returned within two business
days of submission.

### 3.3 Blackout periods

PTO is not normally approved during the last three business days of each fiscal
quarter or during the week of the annual all-hands meeting, except where required
by law.

## 4. Carryover and Separation

### 4.1 Carryover

A maximum of **5 days** of unused vacation may be carried into the
following calendar year. Unused vacation above 5 days is forfeited
on 31 December and cannot be paid out.

### 4.2 Separation

Accrued, unused vacation is paid out at separation where required by law.

## 5. Sick Leave

### 5.1 Accrual

Employees accrue sick leave at 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to a maximum balance of
**5 days**. Sick leave is intended for an employee's own illness or
injury, medical appointments, and care of a family member.

### 5.2 Notification

Employees should notify their manager as soon as practicable on the first day of
absence. Absences of more than three consecutive days may require a note from a
qualified healthcare provider.

### 5.3 Abuse

Sick leave used for purposes other than those described may lead to corrective
action. If you need a recurring or extended arrangement, contact People
Operations to discuss a leave of absence under POL-008.

## 6. Interaction With Other Policies

- Public holidays are covered under POL-002.
- Extended medical and family leave is covered under POL-008, not this policy.
- Fully remote and hybrid schedules are covered under POL-003.

## 7. Questions

Contact People Operations at people-ops@acme.example for questions about accrual
balances or a declined request.
