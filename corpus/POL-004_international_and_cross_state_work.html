POL-004 International and Cross-State Work

Document ID: POL-004
Owner: People Operations and Legal
Applies to: Employees assigned to a United States work location

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs work performed from a location outside the employee's assigned work location, including another state and another country. It does not apply to business travel, which is covered by POL-005.

1.1 Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to employees whose assigned work location is within the United States. Employees based outside the United States are covered by local People Operations arrangements and are not governed by the duration thresholds in section 2.

1.2 How international destinations are treated

For employees covered by this policy, any destination outside the United States is treated as international, regardless of the specific country. The duration thresholds in section 2 apply to every such destination. This policy does not approve or exclude individual countries; it sets a single rule for all non-US destinations. Destinations subject to comprehensive trade sanctions, and destinations on the Legal-maintained prohibited travel list, are handled under section 4.

2. Duration Thresholds

2.1 Up to 30 consecutive days

Manager approval is sufficient. Total time worked away from the assigned location should not exceed 60 days in a rolling 12-month period without further review.

2.2 More than 30 consecutive days

People Operations and Legal approval are both required before travel is booked. Approval is documented in the People Operations portal and normally states an end date.

2.3 More than 90 consecutive days

Requests are normally declined. A longer arrangement is treated as a permanent relocation and is handled through a separate process, not this policy.

3. Tax and Payroll

3.1 Nexus and withholding

Work from another state or country may create a payroll registration obligation. Employees must submit a request at least 30 days before travel so payroll can assess withholding.

3.2 Employment law

Some locations impose local employment obligations on Acme Corp. Where those obligations cannot be met, the request is declined.

4. Restricted and Not Approved Locations

The following are not approved for any duration without Legal approval, and in most cases are not approved: countries subject to comprehensive trade sanctions, and countries on the Acme Corp prohibited travel list maintained by Legal. Employees with a need to travel to a restricted location should contact Legal directly.

5. Security Requirements While Working Away

  1. Connect only through the Acme Corp VPN with multi-factor authentication.
  2. Do not work from public or shared devices.
  3. Do not store Acme Corp data on local drives, removable media, or personal cloud accounts.
  4. Use a privacy screen where the workspace is overlooked.
  5. Report a suspected incident within 24 hours under POL-006.

6. Request Procedure

  1. Submit a work-from-elsewhere request with destination, start date, end date, and reason.
  2. Manager reviews within two business days.
  3. For requests over 30 days, People Operations and Legal review within five business days.
  4. The decision is recorded with an expiry date. Extensions require a new request.

7. Consequences of Unapproved Work

Working from an unapproved location may result in tax liability for the employee, loss of system access, and corrective action. If your circumstances change after approval, notify People Operations immediately.
