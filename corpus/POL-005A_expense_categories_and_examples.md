# POL-005 Annex A: Expense Category Schedule and Worked Examples

**Document ID:** POL-005-A
**Owner:** Finance
**Parent policy:** POL-005 Expenses and Reimbursement

This annex lists the reimbursement limits in one place and works through the
judgement calls that generate the most questions. It restates POL-005 and does
not change it.

## A.1 Category Schedule

| Category | Limit | Receipt required | Pre-approval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home office stipend | $500 one-time | Yes | On remote approval |
| Home internet | $50 per month | Yes | No |
| Peripheral equipment | Up to $150 | Yes | Manager |
| Laptop | Issued by IT | n/a | No |
| Domestic lodging | $250 per night | Yes | Over $1,000 |
| International lodging | $350 per night | Yes | Over $1,000 |
| Domestic meals | $75 per day | Over $25 | Over $1,000 |
| International meals | $110 per day | Over $25 | Over $1,000 |
| Ground transport | Actual, reasonable | Over $25 | Over $1,000 |
| Personal vehicle | IRS standard mileage rate | Log | Over $1,000 |
| Parking and tolls | Actual | Over $25 | Over $1,000 |
| Continuing education | $1,500 per year | Yes | Manager |
| Wellness | $600 per year | Yes | No |
| Client entertainment | Actual, reasonable | Yes | Director |

The general pre-approval threshold is $1,000 for any single expense (POL-005 1).
The lower category thresholds above apply where they are stricter.

## A.2 Home Office Set-Up

An employee joins fully remote and buys:

| Item | Cost | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desk | $320 | Stipend |
| Ergonomic chair | $410 | Stipend |
| Monitor | $180 | Stipend via IT |
| Webcam | $95 | Peripheral, manager approval |
| Desk lamp | $60 | Stipend |
| **Total claimed** | **$1,065** | |

The first $500 comes from the one-time stipend (POL-005 2.1). The remaining $565
is not reimbursable because furniture beyond the stipend is excluded (POL-005
2.3). The webcam is a peripheral under $150 and may be expensed separately with
manager approval (POL-005 3). The monitor is ordered through IT, not expensed.

## A.3 Commute Compared With Work Travel

| Situation | Reimbursable? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary commute to the assigned office | No | Not a business expense |
| Travel to a client site above the normal commute | Yes, the excess | Business travel |
| Overnight stay to attend a morning meeting | Yes | Lodging under POL-005 4.2 |
| Driving to the office from a remote home | No | Treated as a commute |
| Travel booked to attend a conference | Yes | Advance booking expected |

Where a business trip is combined with personal days, only the business portion
is reimbursed (POL-005 4.4). Extending a stay and claiming the extra nights is
not permitted.

## A.4 A Worked Travel Claim

Three days of domestic travel to a conference:

| Item | Cost | Limit applied | Reimbursed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flight, economy, 4 hours | $410 | Economy standard | $410 |
| Hotel, 2 nights | $560 | $250 per night | $500 |
| Meals, 3 days | $215 | $75 per day | $215 |
| Rideshare, airport | $62 | Reasonable | $62 |
| In-room film | $18 | Excluded | $0 |
| **Total** | **$1,265** | | **$1,187** |

The hotel exceeds the nightly limit by $60, so the employee absorbs $60. The
in-room film is excluded under POL-005 4.4. Because the claim is above $1,000,
manager pre-approval is expected before booking.

## A.5 Common Exclusions

Not reimbursable, regardless of amount:

1. Rent, mortgage, or general household utilities.
2. Traffic fines, parking penalties, and towing charges.
3. Personal travel extensions and seat upgrades outside POL-005 4.1.
4. Alcohol beyond a reasonable amount at an approved client event.
5. Travel insurance bought for personal reasons.
6. Gym equipment, where a separate wellness allowance exists under POL-007.

## A.6 Submission Checklist

Before submitting a claim:

1. Confirm every item has an itemized receipt where required.
2. Confirm the cost centre or project code is correct.
3. Confirm the expense is within 30 days of the date incurred.
4. Confirm manager approval where the amount or category requires it.

Claims missing a receipt for an item at or above $25 may be returned without
payment (POL-005 5).
