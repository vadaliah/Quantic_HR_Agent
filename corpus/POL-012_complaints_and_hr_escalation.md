# POL-012 Complaints and HR Escalation

**Document ID:** POL-012
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees and contractors

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how to raise a workplace complaint, how Acme Corp handles
it, when a matter is escalated to an HR case, and what confidentiality and
protection from retaliation apply.

## 2. What Counts as a Complaint

1. Harassment or discrimination under POL-011.
2. Retaliation for reporting a concern.
3. A safety or security concern.
4. A dispute about pay, classification, or a leave decision.
5. A policy breach by a colleague or manager.

## 3. Informal Resolution

Where the matter is not serious and the employee is comfortable doing so, the
employee may raise it directly with the person concerned, or ask their manager to
facilitate a conversation. Informal resolution is not appropriate for harassment,
discrimination, or safety concerns.

## 4. Formal Complaint

### 4.1 How to raise it

Submit the complaint in writing through the People Operations portal, or by email
to hr-cases@acme.example. The complaint should describe what happened, when, who
was involved, and any witnesses.

### 4.2 Confirmation and timeline

People Operations acknowledges a formal complaint within **two business days** and
completes an initial assessment within **five business days**.

## 5. Triage and Escalation

| Category | Route | Target response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Policy question | Answered directly with policy citation | 2 business days |
| Process or pay dispute | People Operations case | 5 business days |
| Harassment, discrimination, retaliation | Escalated case, People Operations lead plus Legal | 1 business day to acknowledge |
| Safety or security threat | Immediate escalation to Security and People Operations lead | Same day |

## 6. Investigation

1. An investigator is assigned who has no conflict of interest.
2. Interviews are held with the complainant, the respondent, and witnesses.
3. Relevant records and messages may be reviewed, consistent with POL-006.
4. Both parties are informed of the outcome in writing, to the extent permitted
   by law and privacy obligations.
5. The investigation normally concludes within 30 calendar days. Where it cannot,
   both parties receive a written status update every 14 days.

## 7. Interim Measures

Where a serious concern is raised, Acme Corp may adjust schedules, reporting
lines, or system access during the investigation. Interim measures are not a
finding and are not disciplinary.

## 8. Confidentiality

Information is shared only with those who need it to assess, investigate, or
resolve the matter. Anonymity cannot be guaranteed where an investigation
requires the respondent to know the substance of the concern.

## 9. Outcomes

Outcomes may include no further action, coaching, a written warning, a performance
plan, reassignment, or termination. Where a policy change is needed, People
Operations records it in the policy review log.
