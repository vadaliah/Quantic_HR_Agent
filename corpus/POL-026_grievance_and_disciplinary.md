# POL-026 Grievance and Disciplinary Procedure

**Document ID:** POL-026
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees

This policy sets out how a grievance is raised and how a disciplinary matter is
handled. Conduct itself is in POL-011; complaints about harassment or
discrimination are handled under POL-012, which takes precedence where both
could apply.

## 1. Raising a Grievance

A grievance is a concern about treatment at work. It is raised in writing with
the manager, or directly with People Operations where the concern involves the
manager.

A grievance must be raised within **3 months** of the event, or of the last in a
series of events. People Operations may accept a later grievance where there is a
good reason, and must record the reason.

## 2. Handling a Grievance

| Stage | Who handles it | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Informal | The manager, with People Operations advice | 10 working days |
| Formal meeting | A manager not previously involved | 15 working days |
| Appeal | A director not previously involved | 15 working days |

The employee may be accompanied at a formal meeting by a colleague or a trade
union representative. The accompanying person may speak, but may not answer for
the employee.

The outcome is given in writing, with the reasons and the right of appeal.

## 3. Disciplinary Matters

A disciplinary matter is a concern about an employee's conduct or performance
that may lead to a formal outcome. Performance shortfalls that are being managed
under a performance improvement plan follow POL-014 instead.

Before any formal meeting, the employee must be told in writing what is alleged,
given the evidence, and given at least **48 hours** to prepare. The employee may
be accompanied.

## 4. Outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| No action | The concern was not substantiated |
| Written warning | Recorded, and current for 12 months |
| Final written warning | Recorded, and current for 12 months |
| Dismissal | Ends employment, with notice or pay in lieu |

A first written warning is not usually given for a minor matter where the
employee has no current warning. Gross misconduct may lead to dismissal without
notice, and is decided by a director, not by the manager alone.

## 5. Suspension

Suspension during an investigation is a neutral act and is not a sanction. Where
an employee is suspended, it is for the shortest period needed, is confirmed in
writing within **2 working days**, and the employee is contacted at least every
**10 working days** with an update.

Suspension is paid at the employee's normal rate.

## 6. Records and Confidentiality

Records are kept under the retention period in POL-017 and are shared only with
those who need them. Details are disclosed outside the process only where the
law requires it, or where a witness needs to know.

A concluded written warning expires after 12 months and must not be relied on
after that date.

## 7. Appeals

An appeal must be submitted within **10 working days** of the outcome. It is heard
by someone not previously involved and at a more senior level. The appeal may
uphold, reduce, or increase the outcome; it is not limited to upholding or
overturning.
