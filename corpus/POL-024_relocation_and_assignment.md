# POL-024 Relocation and Assignment

**Document ID:** POL-024
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** Employees relocating or on temporary assignment

This policy covers moving to a new location and temporary assignments away from
the assigned office. Working abroad without relocating is in POL-004; where the
work is done day to day is in POL-003.

## 1. Relocation Compared With Assignment

| | Relocation | Assignment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duration | Permanent or indefinite | Fixed, up to 12 months |
| Home location changes | Yes | No |
| Housing support | Yes, section 3 | No |
| Payroll | Moves to the new location | Stays in the home location |
| Approval | Director and People Operations | Manager, plus People Operations where abroad |

## 2. Approval

Relocation requires director approval and review by People Operations and Legal,
because the employment terms, tax registration and benefits may change. The
review must be complete before the employee commits to a move, and before
anything is booked.

An assignment of more than **90 consecutive days** abroad follows the approval
path in POL-004, not this policy.

## 3. Relocation Support

Where relocation is company-initiated, the company may fund, with prior written
approval:

1. Shipment of household goods, up to a documented quotation.
2. Temporary accommodation for up to **30 days** at the start of the move.
3. A lump sum toward incidental costs, where the department approves one.

Relocation support is not taxable advice: the employee is responsible for
understanding the tax position in the new location, and the company will not
advise on it.

## 4. Housing and Leases

The company does not sign leases, does not act as guarantor, and does not cover
early-termination costs on a lease the employee signed. Where a move is
company-initiated and the employee holds a lease, support under section 3 may be
used toward the termination cost, at the director's discretion.

## 5. Assignment Terms

An employee on assignment keeps their home location, their manager, and their
existing pay and benefits, unless a written change is agreed. Travel and lodging
during the assignment follow POL-015 and POL-005.

An assignment may be ended early by either side with **30 days' notice**, or
immediately where the employee's safety is at risk.

## 6. Failure to Relocate

Where an employee accepts a relocation and then does not move, the company may
recover support already paid, where the employee agreed in writing before the
move. The employee is not asked to repay where the move was cancelled by the
company.
