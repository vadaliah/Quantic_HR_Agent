# POL-028-ER Employee Relations Case Policy

**Document ID:** POL-028
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees

This policy covers how a formal employee relations case is opened, recorded and
closed after a complaint or grievance has been raised. The routes for raising a
concern are in POL-012 and POL-026; this policy covers what happens to the case
itself.

## 1. Opening a Case

A case is opened by People Operations when a concern cannot be resolved
informally, or where a concern alleges harassment, discrimination, or a breach of
POL-011. The person who raised the concern is told that a case has been opened.

A case is recorded with a case reference, the parties, the allegation in
summary, the policy or policies engaged, and the date opened. Access to the case
record is limited to People Operations and, where required, Legal.

## 2. Case Categories

| Category | Example | Expected handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conduct | Bullying, dishonesty | Investigation, then POL-026 |
| Harassment | Unwanted conduct | Investigation, then POL-026 |
| Discrimination | Treatment because of a protected characteristic | Investigation with Legal engaged |
| Retaliation | Treatment after a protected disclosure | Independent investigator, no reporting line |
| Relationship | Conflict that is not misconduct | Mediation, where both agree |

A case may move between categories as facts emerge, and the move is recorded.

## 3. Investigation

An investigation is carried out by someone independent of the matter and with no
reporting relationship to either party. Engagement with an external investigator
requires director approval and is normal where the matter is sensitive or senior.

The investigation covers the allegation, the evidence, and the responses of those
involved. Interviews are recorded as a summary agreed with the person
interviewed, not as a transcript.

## 4. Timeframes

| Stage | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledged to the person who raised it | 2 working days |
| Investigation started | 10 working days |
| Investigation concluded | 30 calendar days, extendable once with a reason |
| Outcome communicated | 5 working days after conclusion |
| Status update where the case runs long | Every 14 days |

Where the case cannot conclude within 30 days, both parties receive a written
status update every 14 days and the record states why.

## 5. Outcomes

A case concludes with one of:

1. The allegation is upheld, wholly or in part.
2. The allegation is not upheld.
3. The matter is resolved by an agreed action, without a finding.
4. The case is closed without conclusion, with the reason recorded, because one
   party withdrew or the evidence was not sufficient to decide.

The outcome is given to both parties separately and in writing. A person who is
not a party is told only what they need to know, if anything.

## 6. Confidentiality

The fact that a case exists, the allegation, and the outcome are Confidential
under POL-006. Case records must not be stored on a personal device and must not
be shared outside People Operations and Legal without the case owner's approval.

A person who withdraws a concern is not penalised. Retaliation against anyone
involved in a case is itself a breach of this policy and is treated as gross
misconduct under POL-026.

## 7. Recording and Retention

A case record is retained for **7 years** from the date of outcome under POL-027.
A summary without personal detail is retained longer, so that patterns can be
reviewed, and the summary must not identify an individual.

## 8. Reporting

People Operations reports case numbers by category and outcome to the executive
team twice a year. The report contains no identifying detail and is not used to
assess an individual's performance.
