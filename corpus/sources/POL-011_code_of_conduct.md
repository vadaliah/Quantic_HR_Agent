# POL-011 Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment

**Document ID:** POL-011
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees, contractors, and agency staff

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets the standards of conduct expected at Acme Corp. It applies to
everyone working for or with Acme Corp, at any work location, and at
company-sponsored events, including online meetings and off-site events.

## 2. Respect in the Workplace

Everyone is expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with courtesy
and professionalism. This includes:

1. Communicating directly and respectfully, including in written channels.
2. Giving and receiving feedback without personal attack.
3. Respecting physical and personal boundaries.
4. Avoiding disruptive behaviour that interferes with others' work.

## 3. Harassment

### 3.1 Definition

Harassment is unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic, including
race, colour, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national
origin, age, disability, or veteran status, where the conduct creates an
intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment or is a condition of
employment.

### 3.2 Examples

Examples include unwelcome comments about a person's body, repeated unwanted
romantic attention, displaying offensive material, mocking an accent or a
disability, and retaliating against a person who raises a concern.

### 3.3 Sexual harassment

Sexual harassment includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual
favours, and other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature. A single severe
incident can constitute harassment.

## 4. Discrimination and Retaliation

Employment decisions are made without regard to any protected characteristic.
Retaliation against a person who reports a concern, participates in an
investigation, or files a complaint is prohibited and is treated as a serious
violation in its own right.

## 5. Drugs and Alcohol

Being under the influence of illegal drugs, or of alcohol while working, is
prohibited. Alcohol may be served at company events in moderation; employees are
expected to behave professionally and to arrange safe transport.

## 6. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose outside employment, board roles, or financial interests
that could conflict with Acme Corp interests to their manager and Legal. Outside
employment must not compete with Acme Corp and must not be performed during
working hours without approval.

## 7. Confidentiality and Company Property

Employees must protect Acme Corp information under POL-006 and use company
property for legitimate business purposes. Intellectual property created within
the scope of employment belongs to Acme Corp.

## 8. Reporting a Concern

1. Report to your manager, to People Operations, or through the anonymous
   reporting line. The anonymous line is available to everyone, including
   contractors.
2. Concerns are reviewed within two business days.
3. Where the concern involves your manager, report directly to People Operations.
4. Retaliation for reporting is prohibited.

Formal complaints are handled under POL-012.

## 9. Consequences

Violations may lead to corrective action up to and including termination.
Conduct that may be unlawful may also be referred to authorities.

## 10. Acknowledgment

All employees acknowledge this policy at hire and annually thereafter.
