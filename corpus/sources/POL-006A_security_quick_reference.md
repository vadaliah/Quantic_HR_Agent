# POL-006 Annex A: Security Quick Reference

**Document ID:** POL-006-A
**Owner:** Security
**Parent policy:** POL-006 Information and Data Security

A one-page reference for day-to-day decisions. Where this annex and POL-006
differ, POL-006 governs.

## A.1 Classification Cheat Sheet

| Data | Level | May be emailed | May be on a personal device | May go in a public AI tool |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Published policies | Public | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Org charts, process docs | Internal | Yes, internally | Yes, via managed app | No |
| Employee records, salaries | Confidential | Only encrypted, internally | No | No |
| Payroll bank details, IDs, keys | Restricted | No | No | No |

If you cannot classify something, treat it as Confidential and ask Security.

## A.2 Do This, Not That

| Situation | Do | Do not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Working from a cafe | VPN plus privacy screen | Open a laptop on a public network without the VPN |
| Sharing a file with a vendor | Expiring link, confidentiality agreement in place | Link set to "anyone with the link" |
| Moving a file to a colleague | Company share with least privilege | USB stick or personal cloud drive |
| Using an AI assistant | Approved tools in the tool register only | Paste customer or employee data into a public chatbot |
| Losing a laptop | Report to Security within 24 hours | Wait to see whether it turns up |
| Receiving a suspicious email | Report and delete without clicking | Forward it to colleagues to ask if it is real |

## A.3 Working Away From the Office

Before travelling outside your assigned location, confirm:

1. The trip is approved under POL-003 or POL-004.
2. You will connect through the VPN with multi-factor authentication.
3. No company data will be stored on a local drive or removable media.
4. You have a privacy screen for any shared or overlooked workspace.
5. You know how to reach Security if something goes wrong.

Company equipment must not be taken to a restricted location (POL-004 4).

## A.4 Passwords and Authentication

- Multi-factor authentication on every company system, without exception.
- Passwords at least 14 characters, unique per service, kept in the company
  password manager.
- No shared accounts, including inside a team.
- Report a suspected credential compromise immediately; do not wait for the next
  business day.

## A.5 Incident Response in Five Steps

1. **Stop.** Do not keep working on the affected device.
2. **Report** to security@acme.example within 24 hours of discovery.
3. **Preserve** the evidence: do not delete the email or wipe the device.
4. **Cooperate** with the Security review and answer questions directly.
5. **Continue** with your work on another approved device.

Good-faith reporting is protected (POL-006 6.3). Concealing an incident is a
violation; reporting one is not.

## A.6 Who to Contact

| Need | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| Suspected incident or lost device | security@acme.example |
| Access request or permission change | Your manager, then the IT portal |
| Policy question | security@acme.example |
| Restricted travel destination | Legal, via People Operations |

## A.7 Quarterly Reminders

- Access reviews run quarterly; managers confirm that each report's access still
  matches the role (POL-006 3.1).
- Departed colleagues lose access on their last working day; managers should
  report departures to Security in advance.
- Security training is refreshed annually and is mandatory.
