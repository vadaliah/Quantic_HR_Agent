# POL-006 Information and Data Security

**Document ID:** POL-006
**Owner:** Security
**Applies to:** All Acme Corp employees, contractors, and agency staff

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how Acme Corp information is classified, stored, shared, and
protected, and what every person with access must do to keep it safe. It applies
regardless of work location, including employees working remotely under POL-003
and those working abroad under POL-004.

## 2. Data Classification

### 2.1 Levels

| Level | Description | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Approved for release | Marketing pages, published policies |
| Internal | For staff, not for release | Org charts, internal process documents |
| Confidential | Limited to those with a need to know | Employee records, salaries, performance notes |
| Restricted | Highest sensitivity | Government identifiers, payroll bank details, security keys, incident reports |

### 2.2 Handling summary

- **Internal** may be shared on company systems only.
- **Confidential** must be shared through a permissioned company share, never a
  personal account, and never over consumer messaging apps.
- **Restricted** is stored only in designated systems, is never copied to a
  local drive or personal device, and is never sent by unencrypted email.

## 3. Access Control

### 3.1 Least privilege

Access is granted by role and reviewed quarterly. Managers must request access
changes when a person's role changes, and People Operations notifies Security on
the last working day of a departing employee.

### 3.2 Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for all company systems. Passwords must
be unique, at least 14 characters, and stored in the company password manager.
Sharing credentials is prohibited, including within a team.

### 3.3 Access from outside the office

Remote and travelling employees must connect through the Acme Corp VPN. Public
or shared computers must not be used to access company systems, and a privacy
screen is required in any workspace that can be overlooked.

## 4. Devices and Removable Media

1. Company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption and automatic screen
   lock at five minutes.
2. Personal devices may access email and calendar only through the managed app
   with device passcode enforcement.
3. Removable media is prohibited unless issued by IT.
4. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to Security within 24 hours.

## 5. Sharing and Collaboration

- Use company file shares with least-privilege permissions; do not use link
  sharing set to "anyone with the link" for Confidential or Restricted data.
- Do not use consumer cloud storage or personal email for company data.
- When sharing with a third party, use an expiring link and confirm that the
  recipient is covered by a confidentiality agreement.
- Do not include Restricted data in screenshots, demos, or recorded meetings.

## 6. Incident Reporting

### 6.1 What to report

Report suspected phishing, a lost device, a misdirected email containing
Confidential data, or any suspected unauthorised access.

### 6.2 How and when

Report to security@acme.example within **24 hours** of discovery. Do not attempt
to investigate independently, and do not delete the evidence.

### 6.3 No-blame reporting

Good-faith reporting of a mistake is protected. Concealing an incident is a
policy violation; reporting one is not.

## 7. AI Tools and Third-Party Services

Company data must not be entered into a public AI assistant or an unapproved
third-party service. Approved tools are listed in the internal tool register.
Where a tool is approved for Confidential data, the register records that scope.

## 8. Retention and Disposal

Records are retained according to the Acme Corp retention schedule. Disposal of
devices is handled by IT, and paper records containing Confidential or Restricted
data are shredded.

## 9. Enforcement

Failure to follow this policy may result in loss of access and corrective action
up to and including termination, and may carry personal legal exposure in some
jurisdictions.
