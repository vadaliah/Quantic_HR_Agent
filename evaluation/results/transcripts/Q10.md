## Gold answer

Requests over 90 consecutive days are normally declined; a longer arrangement is treated as permanent relocation rather than remote work.

## Agent answer

Working abroad for 120 days is not compliant with the policy. Requests to work outside the United States for more than 30 consecutive days require both People Operations and Legal approval before travel is booked. Since you are requesting to work abroad for 120 days, which is more than 90 consecutive days, the request is normally declined. Additionally, some countries are not approved for any duration. You will need to seek approval from both People Operations and Legal before proceeding with the request.
