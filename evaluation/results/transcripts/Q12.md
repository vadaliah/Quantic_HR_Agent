## Agent answer

When working from a cafe or any public location, the following security rules apply:

1. Connect only through the Acme Corp VPN with multi-factor authentication.
2. Do not work from public or shared devices.
3. Do not store Acme Corp data on local drives, removable media, or personal devices.

These rules are outlined in the policy document POL-004, section 5, titled "Security Requirements While Working Away."
