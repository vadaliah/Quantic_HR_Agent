## Gold answer

Any single expense above $1000 needs manager approval in advance; the amount above the limit is not reimbursed without it.

## Agent answer

The policy states that items under $150 may be expensed by the employee and claimed under POL-005. Items above $150 are purchased by IT on a purchase order and are not expensed by the employee. Since the laptop you want to buy is $2000, it falls under the category of items above $150 and must be purchased by IT on a purchase order. You cannot expense it yourself.
