## Gold answer

At least five business days in advance.

## Agent answer

According to the policy document POL-001, section 3.1, employees must submit vacation requests through the People Operations portal and notify their manager. Specifically:

- Requests of fewer than 3 days require manager notification only.
- Requests of 3 days or more require both submission through the portal and manager notification.

Therefore, the notice period for a vacation request depends on the duration of the vacation. For requests of fewer than 3 days, only manager notification is required. For requests of 3 days or more, both submission through the portal and manager notification are required.
